// FAQ
Common Questions
Is it really free to join? ▾
Yes — completely free. No monthly fee, no setup cost. You only pay when you choose to claim a lead. We require a card on file so that claiming is instant, but you're never charged until you click claim.
Are leads exclusive? ▾
Yes. Every lead on Canvassing.ca is exclusive. Once claimed by one professional, it's removed from the marketplace permanently. You'll never be competing with other agents for the same person.
Where do the leads come from? ▾
We use a proprietary network of Canadian data sources that we've built and refined over time. What we can tell you is that every lead is a real Canadian consumer who is actively looking for a service — not recycled, not scraped from a generic list. Our sourcing methods are what keep our lead quality consistently high.
How fast do leads appear? ▾
New leads are added in real-time throughout the day. You'll receive an instant SMS and email the moment a matching lead appears in your postal code area. The average claim time is under 52 seconds.
Is this only for Canada? ▾
Yes — Canvassing.ca is built exclusively for Canadian professionals and serves consumers across all provinces and territories. All billing is in Canadian dollars.
What if a lead's contact info is wrong? ▾
We have a lead quality guarantee. If you claim a lead and the contact information is invalid or unreachable, contact our support team and we'll issue a full credit to your account.
