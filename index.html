// FAQ
Common Questions
What exactly is a kept appointment? ⌄
A kept appointment is one where the homeowner was present and met with you as scheduled. If the homeowner cancels or doesn't answer when you arrive, you are not charged. You only pay for appointments where someone actually showed up and had a conversation with you.
What happens if the homeowner no-shows? ⌄
No charge. If a homeowner cancels with less than 24 hours notice or simply isn't home when you arrive, we do not bill for that appointment. We will also book you a replacement appointment in the same neighbourhood at no additional cost.
How are appointments booked into my calendar? ⌄
We work with your preferred calendar — Google Calendar, Outlook, or any platform that accepts calendar invites. When a canvasser books an appointment at the door, you receive an instant notification with the homeowner's name, address, appointment type, and any notes captured during the conversation.
Can I choose which neighbourhoods you canvass? ⌄
Yes — you define the target. Give us postal codes, specific streets, or a general area and we deploy canvassers there exclusively. Your territory is yours alone — we do not run competing agents in the same neighbourhood at the same time.
What do canvassers say at the door? ⌄
Canvassers are trained on a script we develop together based on your style, your brokerage, and the type of appointments you want. They introduce you by name, explain that you're a local agent helping homeowners understand the current market, and offer a free home evaluation or home evaluation. The conversation is warm and low-pressure — not a pitch.
What does the daily report include? ⌄
Every evening you receive a report showing total doors knocked by street, number of homeowner conversations, appointments booked with full details, any notable interactions that didn't convert to a booking, and canvasser performance metrics. Nothing is hidden — you see the full picture of what happened in your neighbourhood each day.
Is there a contract or minimum commitment? ⌄
No long-term contract. We want to earn your business through results. You can pause or stop your campaign at any time. Our only ask is a short notice period so we can wind down canvasser scheduling in your territory without leaving them stranded mid-campaign.
How is this different from buying online home evaluations? ⌄
Online leads are people who clicked an ad, filled out a form, and have already forgotten about it by the time you call. Our appointments are homeowners who had a real face-to-face conversation, agreed to meet with you, and are expecting your visit. The homeowner's mindset is completely different — and your close rate reflects it.
